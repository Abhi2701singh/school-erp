How to get Razorpay Keys?
  1. Open dashboard.razorpay.com.
  2. Click Account & Settings (⚙️) on the bottom-left sidebar.
  3. Under Website and app settings, click API Keys.
  4. Click Generate Key (or Regenerate Key).
  5. Copy the Key ID and Key Secret and paste them into the form on the left.
  6. Click Save Gateway Configuration.
Testing vs Live: Keys starting with rzp_test_ allow testing without deducting real money. Keys starting with rzp_live_ credit real payments directly into the school's bank account.
What will students see?
